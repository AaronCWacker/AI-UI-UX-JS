Individual animals

Adult female + adult male of the same species, feed, health, a well, and shelter are required. Births stop at capacity. Juveniles grow, winter consumes stored grain, and raids frighten livestock. Gestation and seasons are gameplay abstractions, not real biological time.

Chronicle

Historical grounding

This is a procedural Viking-Age-inspired game archipelago, not reconstructed real geography or a reenactment of particular raids. Town forms draw on longhouses, outbuildings, craft plots, waterfront trade and defended enclosures. Real hauling could involve lowering masts, unloading ballast, carrying poles and lubricated wooden tracks. The game simplifies weight, injuries, town economy and time.

Build provenance

Rebuilt from Aaron’s Skíðblaðnir raiding simulator and Viking Tides v5.1. The r184 renderer / TSL water, cached heights, batching, adaptive quality, pooled effects and capture approach are carried forward; the gameplay core is new. Combat here targets defenders; noncombatants flee. The old menu-only instant combat and captive-trading actions are not retained.
