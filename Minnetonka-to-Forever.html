    A memory quest from Lake Minnetonka to Gates of the Arctic

    Minnetonka
    to Forever

    Larry spent forty years guiding anglers on Lake Minnetonka. Now dementia blurs the water he knows by heart, and Mary, his wife of 45 years, is managing arrhythmia, COPD and a lymphoma in remission. Their kids rebuilt a 1970s Airstream into an amphibious RV. The plan: reach every national park, photograph what lives there, catch what swims there, and send the data home to the researchers who keep them going.
