AARON'S SOMA SIMULACRUM

A live mirror next to a 3D simulacrum: face mesh, body surface, Gaussian splat scanning and constraint rig, all driven by MediaPipe on your camera.

  • Say "record" to start a 60 s MP4 + MP3 capture and "stop" to save it (stored, then downloaded)
  • Speech becomes captions, and you can have them read back in a Google voice when one is available
  • Gestures, expressions and enrolled faces can be bound to macro buttons
  • Exports: GLB (mesh + splats), animated face-take GLB, and 3DGS PLY

Processing stays on this device. Model files load from Google's MediaPipe CDN.
