Aaron's Asclepius Ark

Healer missions aboard the hospital world-ship. Every patient carries a real ICD-10-CM diagnosis with the exactly-right modern workup and a teaching pearl; the treatments are fictional future medicine. Beam into a deck, sweep room after room — every door you open is generated as you arrive — heal everyone you can in 60 seconds, then ride your rocket home to Earth.

Controls

Mouse turns like Doom — just move it; click once to lock it (Esc frees) · [ ] sensitivity · M mouse-turn on/off · any mouse button heals · WASD move · Shift sprint · F phase-dash · hold Mouse/Space heal · 1–4 tools · Q healing pulse · E talk · hold B to board rocket · Tab panel · H hide UI
