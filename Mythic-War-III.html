Four paths to dominion.

Explore. Fly with WASD, Q/E for altitude, Shift to boost. Uncharted land is revealed within sight. Click a province to select it. Drag/swipe to orbit, scroll to zoom. C cycles cameras. Tap explored land on the minimap to warp.

Expand. Claim a revealed province adjacent to your land for 40 gold. Clear demons and gates before claiming. B claims the selected tile.

Exploit. Owned provinces produce gold and lore. Build gold mines for 85 gold, or convert them to mana spires for 85. T builds, R opens research.

Exterminate. Click dragon cards (or 1/2/3) to drop three dragons aimed at the selected province. Z repeats your last stack. Rally redirects your dragons. Space breathes fire; F unleashes your charged cataclysm. Reduce each gate to zero HP, then X seals it for 25 mana when a warden or dragon is nearby. Win with all four gates sealed and at least twelve provinces.

Defend your crown from the demon tide. Five warden lives; respawn or spectate after defeat. P toggles AI command; movement takes control. An AI host continues to the next age after twelve seconds. Real-time cooperative strategy: one shared treasury, eight human slots, AI assistance.

WAKE autojoins through PeerJS; same-browser tabs also connect locally. One elected host simulates the realm and hands off its latest state when leaving. Cross-device play requires reachable WebRTC signaling and compatible network routes. Shared rooms are cooperative, not cheat-proof.
