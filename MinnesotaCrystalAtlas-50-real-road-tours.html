Minnesota Crystal Atlas

Real geography. A little starlight.

A focused Three.js reimagining of Minnesota Crystal Cities, with a road-following 50-stop circuit and a separate blood-cell scene.

Drag Orbit · right-drag panWheel Zoom / pinch on touchSpace Play or pause the tour← → Previous / next stopC Orbit / chase / windshieldH State overviewB Atlas / cellsF Fullscreen

Choose a city from the menu or click its crystal/minimap marker. Play follows the stored road polyline. Dragging frees the camera; the tour can keep moving. Road time is compressed by the speed selector, with a short pause at each stop. All 50 stops form a closed loop beginning in Mound.

What is real?

  • USGS 3DEP elevation, resampled to 321×321. At this statewide resolution, bridges and small landforms are not resolved. The relief slider changes displayed height only. Contours are 50 metres.
  • Census 2024 municipal representative points, plus a TIGERweb state boundary. Road stops are snapped to drivable roads; city beacons mark the representative points.
  • OSRM routing on OpenStreetMap: 50 connected legs, 3,767 km, 52.5 estimated driving hours. The route includes connecting local roads as well as highways. It is a stored scenic itinerary, not live traffic or turn-by-turn navigation.
  • MnDOT trunk highways provide the statewide reference network (January 2020 snapshot). That background network is separate from the routed circuit.
  • USGS NHD supplies 31 selected lake features. Outlines are generalized and coverage is partial, including separately named bays of Lake Minnetonka.

Coordinates use a local equirectangular display centred at 46.4° N, 94.4° W. Distances shown come from the road router. Roads are draped over the coarse terrain. Crystal structures and the vehicle are symbolic, enlarged to remain visible.

Inside the cell view

The red cell is a closed biconcave disc. The white cell shown is a neutrophil, one of several white-cell types. Engulfed cargo enters a phagosome separate from the connected lobed nucleus. Models, colors and timing are illustrative. American Society of Hematology: blood basics.

Geography is bundled in this single HTML file. Internet access is needed for the pinned Three.js CDN modules; no API key, live route request, account or build step is needed. City descriptions are editorial. Data retrieved 19 September 2026.
