🦴 Auto-rig from the footprint

The rigger looks at where a model touches the ground. It grids the contact band, finds the separate contact islands, measures each column above them, and calls each one a wheel, a leg or a post. Two legs is a biped, four is a quadruped, three or more posts is furniture, round columns are a rolling chassis. It then splits the triangles into those columns and hangs each on its own pivot, so a static GLB starts walking or rolling.

🏝️ Island sim

📜 The method

1 Build the body as one kinematic chain, not a pile of meshes.
2 Derive every landmark from the head canon so proportions stay legal.
3 Drive the gait from a single phase: pelvis leads, spine counters, head holds gaze.
4 For imported models, read the ground contact band rather than guessing a skeleton.
5 Cluster the contacts, measure each column, classify wheel / leg / post.
6 Split the triangles into those columns and re-hang them on pivots.
7 Drive the pivots: wheels roll by arc length, legs swing in anti-phase.
8 Select, group, transform and stage the results like a modelling app.
9 Ride the CANON-25 ring around anything selected and export GLB.
10 Score the round trip on silhouette agreement, not on vibes.

🪞 Mirror mediapipe → chain → body

Live pose drives the homunculus chain directly, so the figure is the skeleton. Assign any imported GLB as a body and it binds to that same chain — skinned for organic meshes, rigid for hard-surface. Keys: M mirror N mocap on/off.
