    Choose your trial

    1. Deal 3 fills your hand from the remaining angel deck. Used cards never return. Shuffle redraws your unplayed hand; three uses per match.
    2. Tap a card. Its 45° turn means “in play.” Choose 🧠 insight, 💚 compassion, or ⚔️ resolve. Matching sentence icons gain +2 stance.
    3. Each of your three sentences meets one shadow sentence. Match its needed response icon for +2 counter. Higher strength wins that clash.
    4. Most clashes wins both cards. Tied clash counts use total strength; an exact tie favors light on odd encounters and shadow on even ones.
    5. Play all 22 encounters. The side holding more of the 44 cards wins; 22–22 is a draw. Points reward wins and thoughtful counters.
    6. WAKE co-op: one shared hand and battlefield. Players take turns choosing angels. The host controls the rule variant. Solo disconnects from WAKE.

    Shadow characters and their dialogue are original fictional personifications of adversity. Their statements are obstacles to challenge. Card outcomes describe this game, not predictions about your life.
