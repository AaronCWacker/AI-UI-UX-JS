Your atelier, your way.

1. Match the client's silhouette, fabric and colour family. 2. Draft with four pattern handles. 3. Trace glowing seam points in order, then debut your look.

Camera: Drag or swipe to orbit; wheel or pinch to zoom. C cycles cameras. WASD moves your model; Q/E rotates, Z/X moves up/down in Fly through. E also greets a nearby designer or client. Shift moves faster. Space poses. P toggles AI designer.

Floor map: Tap an open place to reposition your model; tap a designer to focus the camera. In Follow and Fly through, mobile movement buttons appear.

AI designer: Continuously drafts, stitches, debuts and takes the next commission. Any design edit or movement returns control to you.

Procedural cloth motion approximates fabric; this is a game, not a manufacturing pattern or cloth solver. Clients are fictional. Media and saved collections remain on this device. No public posting happens automatically.
