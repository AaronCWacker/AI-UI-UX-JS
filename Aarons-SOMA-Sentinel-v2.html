Enroll records a 3.5 s password gesture: face geometry, bone and finger ratios, a hand trajectory, skin and iris tone. It also exports reference GLB A with that template embedded. Login repeats the gesture along with a random liveness challenge, scores it against the reference and exports GLB B carrying the result. The challenge plus parallax and micro-motion checks make photo and replay attacks harder. Webcam landmarks are not secure biometrics, though: a lookalike, a deepfake fed through a virtual camera, or a 3D mask can get through. Use this as a second factor, never as the only one. Everything stays on this device.

Arms, legs and fingers are solved as a 3D rig: x and y come from the image, depth from MediaPipe's metric world landmarks, and each bone's length is learned over time and then locked, which stops limbs stretching or flattening. Occluded joints hold their last direction at the learned length. The head shell shares the face mesh's outer ring, so there is no seam between face and head.

AARON'S SOMA SENTINEL v2

A live mirror beside a constrained 3D simulacrum: face mesh with a sealed head shell, a bone-length-locked body and finger rig, Gaussian splat scanning, and a gesture-password identity check.

  • Say "record" to start a 60 s MP4 + MP3 capture and "stop" to save it. You also get an animated, rigged body-take GLB
  • Rigged GLB exports one skinned avatar (47 bones, face + head + body + fingers) with splats and your auth template embedded
  • Auth: enroll a gesture password (GLB A), then log in against it (GLB B) and get a live auth %. Stored videos can be compared too
  • Speech becomes captions. Gestures, expressions and faces can be bound to macros

Processing stays on this device. Model files load from Google's MediaPipe CDN.
