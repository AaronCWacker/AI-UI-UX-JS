Why this version is simpler · app audit
Privacy, graphics & compatibility

No account, analytics, AI API, public room, or automatic publishing. Scripted autoplay is a rehearsal director, not an AI model or a legal officiant. Browser voices marked “online” can send speech text to their provider; local voices are used by default.

The embedded WebGL engine starts without a download. Three.js is an optional rendering upgrade fetched from a public CDN. The same modeled scene is used in both. This is a fictional Minnesota-like lakeshore, not survey data or a reconstruction of a private property. Figures are procedural characters, not real people's likenesses.
