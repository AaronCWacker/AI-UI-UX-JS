    1. Draw up to three. Select an angel, then Top, Middle or Bottom. You can place up to two angels per lane. The first is the front line.
    2. Match sentence icons to terrain: Top 🧠, Middle 💚, Bottom ⚔️. Every third beat cycles back to the first sentence. A matching sentence gives +2 attack.
    3. Build a formation: guardians defend; menders heal their lane; choristers buff allies; seers pierce armor; strikers cleave; banishers strip armor. Swap front and back with ⇄. Select a deployed angel to move it; healthy angels can return to your hand.
    4. Choose a stance: 🧠 Insight pierces 1 armor. 💚 Compassion heals 2 every third beat. ⚔️ Resolve gives +2 attack but takes +1 damage.
    5. Battle plays all lanes together. Demons advance from the right. Units fight until a side falls or twelve beats pass. Health persists if both sides survive.
    6. Clear a lane: surviving angels march right, recharge fully and return to the bottom of your draw deck. After a kill, a return earns mastery; every two returns adds +1 attack and +2 health, up to level 3.
    7. Graveyards are permanent. Killed cards never return. Surviving unopposed demons breach the sanctuary and return to their draw deck.
    8. Victory: defeat all 22 demons. Defeat occurs at 0 sanctuary health, loss of all angels, or wave 40. WAKE players alternate planning turns on the same battlefield.

    The shadow characters are fictional personifications of adversity. The sentences remain reflective game themes, not predictions.
