🩺 Holo Doctor AI

Nerbert Albert Sherbert is an original hologram doctor living on a sheep farm. He scans a live body pose, overlays anatomy, shrinks a repair craft, and sends you into organ-scale or imaging-scale worlds.

🌿 Landscape

TSL wind bends instanced grass and reeds from the tips while roots stay planted. The lake uses vertex swell + procedural bump, physical IOR/transmission, shoreline foam, and a shared terrain basin.

🕹️ Open world

Farm play is third-person walk/rover/boat/hover. QWE/ASD/ZXC is the primary nine-key driving pad; WASD remains compatible. F interacts, Space uses the current tool, G cycles tool, Tab changes vehicle, V changes camera, and R resets.

🌐 Multiplayer + AI

PeerJS room state syncs human actors and a subset of each player’s TF.js agents. The built-in TF.js policy is trainable and replaceable with a compatible local or URL model.

🎮 Micro flight

Inside anatomy/body twin: QWE/ASD/ZXC gives true 6-DOF keypad flight: W/X forward/reverse, A/D left/right, Q/E rising diagonals, Z/C sinking diagonals, S air-brake. Shift boosts; drag the 3D view to aim/look; top-level pages may also use pointer lock; Space fires the repair tool.

🩻 Scan

SCAN starts local camera tracking. The body model is a visualization of landmarks only. No diagnosis is attempted.

🧬 Living Body Atlas

BODY combines your camera-derived twin with the connected Cardio Atlas skeleton, heart, vessels and organs plus the Cancer Atlas breast, skin, lymph, metastasis and cellular worlds. NIfTI remains optional advanced scenery.

⚕ Treatment quests

Stops deliberately teach mechanisms, evidence concepts, and limitations rather than recommending care for the player.
