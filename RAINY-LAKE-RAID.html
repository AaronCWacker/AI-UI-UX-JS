📜 Frontier lore Rainy Lake country

No famous hauntings up here — just the quiet kind of lore. Rainy City, a boomtown that lived and died with the 1894 gold rush. The Little American Mine headframe still standing on its island. The Kettle Falls Hotel, the border inn you can only reach by water. A logging camp with its boom ring still afloat, and the Portage des Voyageurs where the fur brigades carried their canoes since 1731. All six stand along the courses — drive or boat past their signs.

🖤 The braid is road-space kinematic: heading is read from the track, never integrated, so the truck cannot spin out or leave the ribbon. Banked corners are carved from real curvature.
