Watch 89 numbers learn to draw edges

This is a real, tiny neural network: a 3 × 3 convolution with 8 channels, ReLU, a 1 × 1 convolution, and sigmoid. It learns from generated shapes and an optional photo example. It is an educational edge model; your original artist networks have about 4.29 million parameters each.

① 📷 Examples36 generated training images teach a local edge rule. Ten separately generated images stay out of training.
② 🧱 ArchitectureNine neighboring grayscale pixels feed eight learned features and one output. Dark is ink; white is paper.
③ 🔁 LearningBackpropagation and Adam update actual weights. Each epoch uses 4,096 sampled patches. Training minimizes binary cross-entropy.
④ 🧪 ValidationThe chart reports mean squared error on fixed training and held-out patches. Lower is better on this task, not a promise of artistic quality.
⑤ 💾 Model filesONNX contains the computation graph and weights. A checkpoint JSON stores this workshop’s weights, recipe, and metrics.
⑥ 🌱 LimitsA 3 × 3 view cannot understand buildings or faces. More varied data, larger receptive fields, and separate photo evaluation would be needed.

Training uses generated shapes only. Add an unfiltered neural result to try teacher/student learning. Photo examples stay in this tab.
