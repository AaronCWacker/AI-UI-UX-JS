HOW TO PLAY

Escape each electrified maze sector across 24 Minnesota cities. Every screen sits on that city's real elevation — contour lines, hillshade, lakes.

W A S D move · ← ↑ → ↓ fire 8-way (combine for diagonals) · Space fire facing · Enter start/pause · O autopilot · R record MP4 · T screen capture · M music · V voices · G gallery · X mixer · I telemetry · F fullscreen

Touch: left stick moves, right stick aims and fires. Gamepad: left stick move, right stick fire, A fire, Y autopilot, Start pause, Back record.

Rules: walls are electrified. Touching a robot, a wall, a robot bolt or the Warden costs a life. Robots die walking into walls, each other, or each other's bolts. Destroy every robot for a sector bonus. Linger and the Warden comes bouncing through the walls — it cannot be destroyed. Extra life every 5,000 points. Robot squads change color and get deadlier as you travel.

Power-ups: 🛡️ shield (absorbs one hit) · ⚡ rapid fire · 🔱 spread shot · 💥 EMP blast · ⏳ time dilation (slows robots, delays the Warden).

Recording: 🎥 records the game canvas with all game audio (music, SFX, robot voice FX, your MP3) to MP4 at up to 4K. 🖥️ captures screen/tab with tab audio, which also includes the spoken robot voices. Clips save to 🎞️ Gallery; use 🎛️ to fit a clip to an MP3 for posting on X (2:20 cap).

Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles (Terrarium encoding, from USGS/SRTM sources), fetched live at zoom 12 and cached. When offline the badge reads SYNTH and a stand-in terrain is used.
