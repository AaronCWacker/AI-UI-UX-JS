Primitive legend

Champion genome

Each sub-organism grows inside its own membrane from the same recipe, differing only by a per-cell hash. Where two of them push tips within reach of a shared border they fuse — and a fused pair moves sustenance from the fatter to the leaner and copies genes from the fitter to the weaker. Connection is therefore both a lifeline and a channel for inheritance. G forces a generation.
