Audio, dependencies & privacy
Three.js r186 loads from jsDelivr. The MP3 encoder loads lamejs 1.2.1 from a CDN inside a worker only when recording starts. These requests disclose ordinary connection metadata to their hosts, not your recordings. Audio encoding, notes, art, and video stay in your browser. Browser-provided online voices have their own provider behavior. Narration and imported files may have licensing or platform restrictions on redistribution.
