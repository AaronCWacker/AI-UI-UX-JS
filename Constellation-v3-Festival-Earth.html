🚁 Ways to explore

Travel controls

W / ↑ accelerate · S / ↓ brake, then reverse · A / ← left · D / → right · Space handbrake · Shift boost · R reset · C/V camera · H AI drive · Tab next vehicle. On foot: A/D strafe, mouse/right pad look. In flight: Q/E down/up.

Touch: steer with the left pad, or the arrow buttons. Hold the throttle, brake or drift button; cruise toggles automatic throttle. Boat racing always uses the Lake Minnetonka GIS shoreline. Water depth is visual, not a bathymetric survey.

Rescue arcade

A/D aligns your ship. Space breaks a saucer beam, then catch the falling animal. Five lives, evolving waves, a recoverable buddy ship and a saved ACW top ten.

Performance budget

⚙️ World & diagnostics

Realism controls

Growth is disabled for mapped cities; measured footprints and elevations are not inflated by a simulated year.

Geographic district

A district pack retains its road coordinates, footprints and sampled terrain. No download is required to reopen the core geometry. The proving ground is a separate, clearly fictional test track—not a replacement city.

Landing automatically loads a bounded DEM / OSM district. Gray gaps mean missing elevation. Real road alignments and building footprints come from OSM. Building heights without height tags, lane widths without width tags, and bridge clearances are estimates.

Local navigation

Location is requested only by this button. A direct bearing is not road navigation; no location is uploaded.
