Aaron's Ambulance and Airlifts

An ambulance racing squadron with Roadster-style cold-gas boosters and an airlift squadron, racing patients from Lake Minnetonka through Eden Prairie to Methodist, North Memorial, HCMC and Regions. Every hill, lake, street and tower is generated from code at runtime.

or pick a mode to start fresh

Real hospital names, addresses and services are references; patients, calls, scores and routes are fictional game designs, not medical guidance. Travel distance is compressed. Roads west of Hopkins follow MetroGIS centerlines and the Minnetonka shoreline follows MN DNR mapping as compact vectors; everything else is procedural.
