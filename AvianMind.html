🪽 Take a creature from idea to open sky.

  1. Press the large Play button: draw three companions and start a calm autopilot ride. Pause holds your formation. The Squad panel contains 40 editable archangel slots.
  2. Pick a bird, adjust its anatomy, then Generate.
  3. Import multiple GLBs or add primitives. Select a part in the list or world; use its gizmo to assemble it.
  4. Fly the entire assembly. W/S forward/back, A/D turn, Q/E down/up, Z/X bank, Space boost. Touch pads work on mobile.
  5. Save stores the assembly recipe and assets in IndexedDB. Export GLB downloads the assembled current pose. Imported GLB animation plays in the studio.
  6. Browser copies auto-join the common room with distinct names. Use Leave to disconnect; files are offered explicitly. A receiving pilot chooses Download, then can add or fly the model.
  7. Load music and video from Assets, then record a 60-second composite. The capture includes the 3D world, video inset, and both audio tracks.

PeerJS uses a public signaling service. Some firewalls require a TURN relay, which this standalone version does not provide. A new host is elected if the current one leaves.
