Controls & expedition guide

WASD / arrows: move. Shift: dash. E / Space: free befriending. 1–3: select companion. Alt+1–3: swap and cast. Tab: swap squad. Hold Q: aim, release: cast. C: throw capture pods. F: interact. G: glide. V: climb. T: track rare. M: map. J: codex. Esc: close panels.

Guardian: place persistent fields to deplete its break meter. Save energy to use more skills during its seven-second opening. Defeat means friendship; the guardian returns after one minute. Open its reward with F.

Weather forms appear only during their matching weather. Follow a friend to share treasure; each explorer can claim each chest only once per room session. Reloading starts a new explorer identity.
