⚕ Safety

Educational simulator only. Camera landmarks and uploaded imaging are not diagnostic. The app does not identify disease, select therapy for a person, prescribe drugs, or replace a licensed clinician or radiologist.

For real clinical decisions, use the actual diagnosis, imaging interpretation, labs, pathology/biomarkers, medication list, contraindications, goals, and current professional guidance.

🩺 Holo Doctor AI

Nerbert Albert Sherbert is an original hologram doctor living on a sheep farm. He scans a live body pose, overlays anatomy, shrinks a repair craft, and sends you into organ-scale or imaging-scale worlds.

🌿 Landscape

TSL wind bends instanced grass and reeds from the tips while roots stay planted. The lake uses vertex swell + procedural bump, physical IOR/transmission, shoreline foam, and a shared terrain basin.

🕹️ Open world

Farm play is third-person walk/rover/boat/hover. WASD moves, mouse looks, F interacts, Space uses the current tool, G cycles tool, Tab changes vehicle, V changes camera, and R resets.

🌐 Multiplayer + AI

PeerJS room state syncs human actors and a subset of each player’s TF.js agents. The built-in TF.js policy is trainable and replaceable with a compatible local or URL model.

🎮 Micro flight

Inside anatomy/imaging: W/S forward-back · A/D strafe · Q/E down-up · Shift boost · mouse look · Space fires the repair tool.

🩻 Scan

SCAN starts local camera tracking. The body model is a visualization of landmarks only. No diagnosis is attempted.

🧬 Imaging

Load NIfTI data to turn voxel intensity into a 3D point world. Threshold changes what is visible. This is exploratory visualization, not medical interpretation.

⚕ Treatment quests

Stops deliberately teach mechanisms, evidence concepts, and limitations rather than recommending care for the player.
