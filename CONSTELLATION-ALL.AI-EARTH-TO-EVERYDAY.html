🚁 Ways to explore

Travel controls

W/S forward or brake · A/D strafe on foot, steer in vehicles · Q/E yaw · Z/X descend/rise · Shift boost · Space fire in rescue · V first/chase camera · H hide HUD · Esc release pointer.

Touch: left pad moves; right pad looks. Vehicles remain grounded; boats remain in the procedural water zone. Whale mode is fantasy in freshwater.

Rescue arcade

A/D aligns your ship. Space breaks a saucer beam, then catch the falling animal. Five lives, evolving waves, a recoverable buddy ship and a saved ACW top ten.

🧠 Living model bay

Each actor has a model, instructions, memory and a browser voice. Local ONNX engines run in workers; identical model settings share one engine to save memory. Downloads start only when you press Load.

Ready · demo agents are scripted, not loaded language models.

Shared mission

Trusted local / remote endpoint

Only agents set to “Endpoint” send prompts there. Use a trusted proxy with server-side credentials. No provider secret is saved in this app.

📦 Cast & model bay

Drop self-contained GLBs or choose several below. Skeleton-safe clones, metre-scale fitting, animation selection, ground snapping and per-agent character assignment.

The optional robot is Three.js’s RobotExpressive demo asset. Imported models stay local. GLB is a 3D asset; it does not contain the actor’s language model.

The procedural crew is already present at the landing plaza.

Performance budget

GLBs are not automatically decimated. Large textures and meshes can exceed phone memory. Start with one character, then add more. Facial movement is an approximate talk envelope, not phoneme-accurate lip sync.

🎵 Music & movie studio

Soundtrack

Capture

Idle · MP4 first; the actual codec is shown when recording starts.

Scene movies mix the MP3 and optional microphone, not browser speech. To include voices, use Tab / screen and share tab audio where supported. No duplicate soundtrack is mixed when tab audio is present. Unsupported MP4 encoders produce an honestly labeled WebM file.

In-world cinema

Play an imported MP4 from the gallery to put it on the plaza’s cinema screen. Imported videos are muted by default to avoid echo.

⚙️ World & diagnostics

Realism controls

Growth is a procedural scenario, not a reconstructed historical census. The original source’s crystal future emerges after 2050.

Survey district

Not loaded. Procedural ground, blocks and water are explicitly stylized. Survey mode replaces the city core with real OSM ways; missing heights are estimated. Public services can be unavailable.

Local navigation

Location is requested only by this button. A direct bearing is not road navigation; no location is uploaded.

Evaluate this device

FPS, frame p95, draw calls, triangles, asset counts, errors and codec support.
