✦ A constellation with receipts

Gold means “Follows you” appeared in a captured profile card. Cyan means follower status was not shown. A badge is evidence from a snapshot, not a live account check.

Each person is counted once per source post. The count beside their name is distinct captured posts liked. More observed posts make their beacon larger. Camera paths, particles, and orbits are decorative; lines connect people to Aaron and never imply friendships between them.

Start with the supplied 37-person snapshot, then import the Likes pages from your other recent posts. Captures may be incomplete. This measures observed engagement, not every like on your account.

Use ☆ to build a shortlist. Search and filters affect the scene and roster. Tour moves through the visible people. Dragging the camera stops a tour; motion can be paused. Keyboard: ← / → next person, H overview, T tour.

Data saves in this browser when storage is available. Export JSON for a durable backup. No X login or API key is required. The app imports your captures; it does not fetch live likes or alter X Lists.

Clips capture the 3D canvas for 15 seconds, up to 50 fps, without audio. The browser chooses supported MP4 or WebM. PNG captures the same scene. Three.js loads from a pinned CDN and needs an internet connection.
