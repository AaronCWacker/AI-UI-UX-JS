AMM · Working With the Couple

Minnesota reference, checked September 12, 2026. The currently published statutes require an officiant to be at least 21 and registered with a county local registrar (§517.04). Each applicant must declare that they take the other as spouse before an authorized officiant and two attending witnesses (§517.09). At least two signing witnesses must be 16 or older; the completed certificate must be filed with the issuing county’s local registrar within five days (§517.10). Ordination alone should not be treated as confirmation of Minnesota registration.

§517.04 · Authorized officiants · §517.09 · Declarations and witnesses · §517.10 · Certificate and return

Verify the current law and issuing registrar’s instructions for the actual wedding location. Poetry is not a substitute for legal requirements. “I do; I take you as my spouse” is offered as a clear response cue, not guaranteed legal advice for every jurisdiction.
