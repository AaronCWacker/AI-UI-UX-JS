Island sim

The method, in order

1 Draw the body from a measurement vector, not by hand.
2 Lock an A-pose so armpits stay readable in silhouette.
3 Ride an orthographic camera around 12 azimuths at a fixed frustum, so scale is identical in every tile.
4 Add 8 tilted views and one top-down for volume that a ring alone cannot show.
5 Render flat albedo for the ring, lit for the tilts — texture without baked shadows, shape with shading.
6 Append 2× crops of head, hands and feet where texture detail matters most.
7 Pack a contact sheet plus a manifest carrying every camera and every measurement.
8 Hand the six highest-information tiles to TRELLIS.2 and export GLB.
9 Re-render the same 12 cameras from the GLB and score silhouette agreement.
10 Drop the scored asset into the sim and drive past it.

Anything in the scene can go through the same rig — pick it from the target list in the Forge tab, or call FORGE.captureObject(obj) from the console.
