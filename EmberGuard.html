Mouse + keyboard: WASD moves relative to the view. Drag either mouse button to look; Q/E descend/ascend; Shift boosts. Arrow keys look around; Z/X or the wheel zoom. Double-click the terrain (or Shift-click) to travel there. Click or tap the minimap to relocate your player and recenter the camera immediately. Middle-drag or Shift-drag pans framing; Recenter / Backspace clears it. In Fly-through, forward follows your viewing pitch. Speed adjusts movement pace.

iPad: hold and drag the left thumb pad to move, swipe the world to look, pinch to zoom, or move two fingers together to pan. Use UP/DOWN, BOOST and SPRAY with the other hand. Double-tap the ground for a waypoint. Lifting a finger stops that control.

Water paint: hold Space or SPRAY with tool 1. Cyan water droplets cool the fire and paint blue-green pools; keep watering to grow magical young trees. Growth is an accelerated fantasy effect, not a model of real post-fire regeneration. Painted patches and growth are shared with the squad and captured in video.

Control Soul: H toggles ground-level hiking and levitation. R lifts three stones into orbit; R again launches them at a nearby invader (or your aim). B creates a gravity well that gathers and stuns invaders. Wet targets take extra Soul Launch damage. These are scripted arcade forces, not a rigid-body physics engine.
