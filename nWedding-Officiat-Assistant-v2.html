Checking browser storage…

  

  
MP3 audio is encoded locally at 96 kbps mono and checkpointed while recording. No audio upload or transcription. Clips are limited to 60 minutes; start another clip to continue. Keep this page open and the device awake; leaving the page pauses capture when possible. Closing it can lose the tail. Download important recordings—browser data can be cleared, evicted, or lost in private browsing. Storage is local to this origin, not encrypted.

  

  

  

  
Question bookmarks refer to audio time with pauses removed. They are navigation aids, not automatic speaker labels. An interrupted session contains only the chunks that reached storage.
