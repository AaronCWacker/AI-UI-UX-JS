Aaron's Arcana Passages

Three passages · twenty-four cards · one journey that learns as you play
1 Player

Solo journey. Turn on Autopilot and it plays itself, ready for music and recording.

2 Players

Take turns. Duels alternate card by card; voyages and quests alternate by stage.

3 Players

You plus two AI wingmen, Mentat and Volta. They learn from your choices and copy your style, vehicles and routes.

Duel: click a card or press 1–5 · read the mantras · tap Space/click when the rings meet for a Perfect.
Voyage: W/S throttle · A/D steer · Space up/boost · Shift down · 1–4 vehicle · V camera.
Quest: WASD move · Space jab (hold to charge a spin) · Shift dash · treasure lets you pick which deck to draw from.
P toggles Autopilot anywhere. Touch any control and you take over; Autopilot resumes after 8 s idle.
